# Arsip Perjalanan — Dinas ESDM Provinsi Jambi

## Product

An operator workspace for digitizing and summarizing trips that have already taken place across the department. Historical actuals are authoritative. No new-trip approval workflow and no automatic application of current tariffs to historical costs.

## Visual direction

A digital register book. The archive page is one white sheet under a row of year tabs, like the index tabs on a bound ledger: the year is the operator's spine, so it is the first control and always visible with its trip count. Above the register sits a single summary band with one large condensed figure (the year's realised cost) and one completeness bar; no metric-card grid. The navigation is a deep navy sidebar drawn from the Jambi crest, with the crest gold reserved for position markers only (active year tab, active menu item, the row that is open). Green and amber mean completeness and nothing else.

Palette: canvas #EEF1F4, surface #FFFFFF, navy #122744, ink #14273F, action #1F4B75, crest gold #D9A53A, line #DCE3EA, muted #5B6B7C, complete #2E6B4F, draft #9B6A10. One family, Instrument Sans Variable, in two voices: standard width for body and controls, condensed width (86%) at 650 weight for the year tabs, reference numbers, and the summary figure. Tabular figures wherever numbers align. 14px data, 13px meta, 12px table headings in sentence case, 30px page heading, 44px summary figure. Labels left aligned; money right aligned.

Layout: 248px navy sidebar / sticky 64px white topbar / content up to 1480px with 36px gutters. Page heading and actions on the canvas, then year tabs, then the sheet: summary band, register head (title, completeness switch, export and view tools), one filter row (search with `/` shortcut, bidang, bulan), the table with sticky header and in-row expansion, pagination. Selecting rows raises a floating navy dock at the bottom instead of a banner that pushes content. Mobile stacks the same sheet into cards and moves sorting into a select. Detail in a focused wide dialog; form grouped into trip, participants, and historical costs.

Forms are entry sheets of the same book. Every archive dialog (single recap, multiple employees, combined archive) opens with a navy header carrying the kicker in crest gold, the title, the entry-mode switch, and the step tabs as register tabs whose active step connects to the white body with a gold marker. Inside: 17px section headings, 13px labels, 40px inputs with 10px radius and a navy focus ring, numeric inputs right aligned with tabular figures, the cost table as a thin-ruled ledger, and the total as a navy band with a condensed figure. Entry status pills stay green (recorded), blue (details), amber (differs) and only mean the state of the entry. The multi-employee stages keep their two-pane layouts; the selected employee carries the gold left marker.

The flight entry inside the cost sheet is an itinerary, not a pair of folded forms. Two direction tabs, Tiket pergi and Tiket pulang, sit side by side as register tabs with the gold left marker on the open one; each tab reads its own route ("Jambi → Jakarta → Makassar"), its entry status pill, the transit count and the price, so the operator sees both directions before opening either. The open direction is one card: the flights in order down a numbered route line (a navy numeral per flight, a dashed line joining them), each with six fields (origin, destination, airline, date, booking code, ticket number), then a tinted booking block for the application, order ID and the one price that paid for the whole direction. Tambah transit appends the next flight starting from the previous landing city on the same airline and date; Salin rute pergi (dibalik) fills an empty return from the outbound legs reversed, without booking references. The first flight lives in the legacy flat columns and transits in a list beside them, so older archives and the source XLSX layout keep working; exported transits become continuation rows in the same flight columns.

The register layer lives in `src/app/arsip.css` after `globals.css`; the shell overrides there apply to every section, the `ledger-*` classes to the archive page and the Surat Tugas register that reuses them.

Form controls use one 1px navy focus stroke inset over the existing border, without an outer shadow or a size change. This shared rule in `globals.css` covers inputs, textareas, selects, and search boxes across all pages and dialogs. A composite search box owns the focus stroke; its inner input stays borderless. Buttons, tabs, and checkboxes keep one keyboard focus outline without an additional utility ring.

## Surat Tugas

The letter register is the agenda book beside the archive: the same year tabs on the spine, the same white sheet, but organised the way letters are numbered. The sheet opens with a month index rail in Roman numerals I to XII, mirroring the month segment of every ST number; each cell carries its count, empty months stay muted and disabled, and the active cell takes the gold marker on its lower edge. Below it one slim facts band (letters, rekap, employees, realised cost) follows the year and month selection; there is no metric-card grid and no large figure. The register head holds the title with its scope, the sort control, column and density tools, then the search with the `/` shortcut. Opening a row reveals the letter's purpose and a roster of the assigned employees with NIP, position, destination, dates, archive code and cost, each with a button to open the archive, and the letter total in the footer. Rekap without an ST number sit in a quiet panel under the sheet. On phones the page drops its description, the month rail becomes one scrolling row of only the months that hold letters, the facts collapse to the cost line and one row of counts, the search moves above the scope and sort line, an opened card skips the purpose text (the card already shows it) and goes straight to the roster, and each roster row ends with the cost and the open button side by side. The `surat-*` classes live in `src/app/surat-tugas.css`.

## Honorarium

The honour book is the third register of the same family, organised the way Lampiran 3 is: the same year tabs on the spine (tahun anggaran), then one white sheet that opens with an index rail of the five honorarium kinds (pengelola keuangan, pengadaan barang/jasa, perangkat UKPBJ, BMD with and without revenue). Each cell carries the recipient count and the net total for the year; kinds without records stay muted and disabled, the active cell takes the gold marker. Below it the summary band holds one large condensed figure, the net honour paid for the scope, with rekap, recipient and SK counts, and beside it the gross amount over a navy bar that shows the net share against the tax share; no metric-card grid. The register head carries the title with its scope, the Aktif/Terhapus switch, sort, column, density and reload tools; one filter row holds the search with the `/` shortcut and a Dasar SK select. The table lists one recipient per row (name and position, SK number in condensed figures with the kind, the monthly amount times months, bruto, pajak, netto right aligned) and ends in a navy foot row that sums the listed rekap like a printed payment list, with the net total in crest gold. Opening a row reveals three panels: the SK basis, the recipient, and a small calculation ledger ending in a navy net band, then the version line and the actions (edit, add another recipient under the same SK, delete or restore). The form is an entry sheet with the navy head, whose kicker names the SK being edited or extended, and a navy calculation band above the footer that updates as the amounts are typed. On a phone the honour book folds the same way the archive does: the description and the register title step back, the kind rail disappears and the kind becomes a select inside the Filter panel beside the SK select and the sort, the search sits beside the Filter button with its count badge, and each recipient is one card carrying the name and position, the SK reference (with kind and year only when the tabs do not already say them), the calculation line, and the netto with the button that opens the rincian; the row actions wait inside the opened card, and the navy foot row still sums the list. The `honor-*` classes live in `src/app/honorarium.css`.

## Rekap & laporan

The report page is the year-end account of the same book: the same year tabs on the spine (each with its trip count), then one white sheet. The sheet opens with a scope row on the tinted band, holding a bidang select and a bulan select with a reset, so the reader always sees which slice of the archive the figures describe; below it the summary band shows one large condensed figure, the realised cost for that scope, with trip, rekap and employee counts, and beside it the share of rekap that carry an amount. The page's own device is a twelve-column chart of monthly realisation under the summary: navy bars over one continuous baseline, the short value above each bar and the rekap count below, empty months muted and disabled. Every column is a button; choosing one focuses the whole report on that month, the other bars step back to grey, and the chosen column takes the gold marker. Two ledgers follow, realisation per bidang (with a share bar and percentage) and rekap per month; each row name is a button that focuses the report the same way, the focused row is tinted with a gold left marker, and each ledger ends in a navy sum row with the total in crest gold. A breakdown always ignores its own filter, so the chosen month or bidang stays visible among its peers. Months without departures are named in one quiet line instead of seven empty rows. The report follows year, bulan and bidang only; search and completeness belong to the archive register. Export sends exactly the rows in scope. The `laporan-*` classes live in `src/app/laporan.css`.

## Pegawai

The staff page is the book's buku induk, the master roster every archive form draws its names from, organised the way a Daftar Urut Kepangkatan is. The spine carries bidang tabs instead of years, since the operator thinks in org units: units from the model first, other recorded bidang alphabetically, then "Belum dicatat", with "Semua bidang" at the end. The sheet opens with an index rail of golongan I to IV in Roman numerals, each cell carrying its headcount, plus a "Tanpa golongan" cell for ranks in another format; empty groups stay muted and disabled, the active cell takes the gold marker. The summary band holds the headcount as the one condensed figure with facts (with NIP, ever assigned, number of bidang) and beside it a data-completeness bar: a record is complete when name, NIP, jabatan, golongan and bidang are all filled, and green and amber mean only that. The register head has the title with its scope, the Aktif/Terhapus switch, sort, column and density tools; one filter row holds the search with the `/` shortcut, a travel-history select and a completeness select. Rows show the name with jabatan, the NIP in condensed tabular figures, golongan, bidang, and the trip count with the month of the last departure. Opening a row reveals the employee card: an identity list on the left (with names used on older archives when they differ), the travel roster on the right (purpose, dates, destination, ST number, the trip's total cost labelled with its participant count so shared costs are never re-attributed, and a button to open the archive) ending in the total days, and the actions in the foot. No avatars, no metric cards, no status pills. The form is an entry sheet with the navy head whose kicker carries the NIP being edited. The `pegawai-*` classes live in `src/app/pegawai.css`.

## Dokumen

The document page is the book's daftar kelengkapan berkas, the checklist an archivist keeps for the paper each perjalanan must leave behind. Every rekap is one map (folder); every document kind is one kantong (pocket) inside it. The spine carries year tabs with their map counts. The sheet opens with an index rail of the seven document kinds, each cell carrying how many maps already hold that kind and how many still lack it; kinds nobody has or needs stay muted, and choosing a kind narrows the list to the maps that still need it. Below it the summary band holds one condensed figure, the maps whose expected documents are all present, with the photo, PDF and physical-note counts and the number of maps still empty, beside a completeness bar. When a rekap has no explicit required-document list, the four standard travel documents (SPT, SPPD, laporan, kuitansi) are expected by default and the wording says "belum difoto" rather than "wajib"; an explicit list keeps "wajib".

The page's own device is the matrix: rows are maps (ST number in condensed figures, employee names, destination and dates), columns are the seven kinds, cells are marks. A filled navy square with a check (or the count when there are several) means the document is there, an amber-outlined square means it is expected and missing, a faint dot means it is not requested. The last column reads "3 dari 4, kurang Laporan". Clicking a row opens the map in a panel beside the matrix; clicking a cell opens the map at that pocket, scrolls to it and marks it with the gold left marker. The panel has a navy head (kicker "Map berkas" in crest gold, the ST number, names, destination, dates, bidang, the "3 dari 4 berkas baku" line and a link to the rekap), the physical-archive location line, then one pocket per kind, expected pockets first. Each pocket names the document, states its content ("1 foto", "Belum difoto", "Tidak diminta"), shows photo thumbnails, PDF chips and physical-location chips, and carries a Tambah foto button that accepts several JPG, PNG or PDF files at once; the pocket is also a drop target. A quiet link records a physical location for documents that only exist on paper. Missing pockets get a tinted amber card and a filled navy button; the operator never chooses a document type from a dropdown. Thumbnails open a viewer with previous and next within the pocket, a link to open the file in a new tab, and a two-step Hapus dari map. The panel sticks beside the matrix on wide screens and opens as a dialog below 1100px. On phones (760px and under) the kind rail gives way to a "Jenis dokumen" select in the filter row, the summary keeps only the figure, its facts and the bar, and the matrix becomes a card list: each card carries the ST number, names, destination and dates, one row of the seven marks with short labels, and the "0 dari 4, belum ada foto" line, so nothing scrolls sideways. The map opens as a full-screen sheet with the close button in the navy head, and the format hint inside each pocket is hidden so only the physical-location link remains. Deleting a document removes it and records a history entry on the rekap. The `dokumen-*` classes live in `src/app/dokumen.css`, and the sheet keeps `overflow: visible` because WebKit confines sticky panels behind `overflow: clip`.

## Rekap detail dialog

Opening a rekap from any register shows one card of the same book. The navy head names the employee (or the participants), then their NIP, position and bidang, and carries four facts on one line: the ST number in condensed figures, destination, dates with the day count, and the realised cost in crest gold. Tabs sit on a tinted rail with the gold marker; the dialog keeps a fixed height so the rail never jumps between tabs. Ringkasan holds the full purpose text and an administrative list where unknown values read "Belum dicatat" in muted grey, never a dash. The cost tab is a small ledger (component, for whom, amount, total band) followed by payment facts; the employee list appears only when a rekap has more than one participant. Documents, history and the Lampiran 6 facts follow the same list rhythm. The `rincian-*` classes live in `src/app/arsip-rincian.css`.

## Interaction principles

- Search across reference, destination, purpose, participant and NIP.
- Year refers to actual departure year, not data-entry year.
- Unknown historical expenditure remains unknown; zero is an explicit amount.
- Aggregate shared expenses once; never repeat them per participant.
- Import includes worksheet selection, editable column mapping, strict validation, preview and duplicate detection. Imported rows retain source workbook/sheet/row.
- Paper evidence is explicitly labeled as physical archive with a location; never represented as a digital attachment.
- All sample data is fictional and isolated from the real archive.
- Edits use version conflict checks; deletion is recoverable.

# Arsip Perjalanan — Dinas ESDM Provinsi Jambi

## Product

An operator workspace for digitizing and summarizing trips that have already taken place across the department. Historical actuals are authoritative. No new-trip approval workflow and no automatic application of current tariffs to historical costs.

## Visual direction

A calm administrative archive: warm white workspace, white navigation, navy headings, restrained golden-yellow markers drawn from the Jambi crest. The central element is a readable travel register, with clear dates and a small completeness indicator. No ornamental graphs, oversized marketing headlines, or hero artwork.

Palette: canvas #F6F7F9, surface #FFFFFF, ink #172C44, secondary #6B7788, action #234F73, accent #E8B748. Body font Plus Jakarta Sans; numeric amounts use tabular figures. Left aligned labels; money right aligned. 14px body, 12px supporting text, 28px page heading.

Layout: 244px white sidebar / slim breadcrumb header / content with generous 32px gutters. A single horizontal metrics strip above the archive register. Detail in a focused wide dialog; form grouped into trip, participants, and historical costs. Mobile uses a navigation drawer and stacked archive cards.

## Interaction principles

- Search across reference, destination, purpose, participant and NIP.
- Year refers to actual departure year, not data-entry year.
- Unknown historical expenditure remains unknown; zero is an explicit amount.
- Aggregate shared expenses once; never repeat them per participant.
- Import includes worksheet selection, editable column mapping, strict validation, preview and duplicate detection. Imported rows retain source workbook/sheet/row.
- Paper evidence is explicitly labeled as physical archive with a location; never represented as a digital attachment.
- All sample data is fictional and isolated from the real archive.
- Edits use version conflict checks; deletion is recoverable.

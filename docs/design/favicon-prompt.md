# Arsip ESDM favicon

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. A white folder holding a document on navy
represents organized office records. It is an application symbol, not an official seal.

The original generated artwork is saved at `public/art/favicon-archive-source.png`.
Resized exports: `src/app/icon.png` (64px), `src/app/favicon.ico` (16, 32, and 48px),
and `src/app/apple-icon.png` (180px). The favicon preserves transparency; the Apple
export uses a solid navy background. Next.js adds their metadata links automatically.

## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production favicon and home-screen app icon for Arsip Perjalanan, the internal government archive application of Dinas ESDM Provinsi Jambi, Indonesia.
Create ONE final square icon, not a mockup or presentation sheet.
Meaning: organized official records preserved in an archive. The mark is a single bold, beautifully balanced WHITE ARCHIVE FOLDER holding ONE DOCUMENT, on a deep navy rounded square. A short index tab at the upper left makes the folder unmistakable. One upright paper sheet peeks above the folder, with a folded top-right corner formed by simple navy negative space. Only two short, thick horizontal navy lines suggest writing on the document. Unify the paper and folder into one compact geometric silhouette; no thin outline drawing. Distinctive but exceptionally simple, dignified, calm, appropriate to a provincial government office.
Palette: solid midnight navy #122744 and pure white #FFFFFF ONLY.
Composition: perfectly square 1024x1024 canvas. The navy rounded square occupies 96% of the canvas, centered, with corner radius about 20% of its side. The tiny area outside the navy rounded square must be genuinely transparent. The white folder/document symbol occupies about 68% of the canvas width and 65% of its height, optically centered. All important strokes and negative-space gaps must stay thick and legible when reduced to 16x16 pixels.
Style: flat, precise, crisp logo design with clean edges and large simple shapes. Final raster icon art. No texture, paper grain, sketch hatching, gradients, shadows, shine, 3D, surrounding frame, drop shadow, or extra ornaments.
No text, no letters, no initials, no numbers, no arrows, no checkmark, no padlock, no shield, no government seal, no coat of arms, no real ministry logo, no watermark. Do not put the icon on a white presentation background. Only the icon with transparent outer corners.
